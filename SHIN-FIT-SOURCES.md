# Shin Guard Fit

This module is a starting-size guide and user-reported in-store fit checklist. It is not automated image analysis or a safety certification.

## Sources checked September 21, 2026

* Bauer Canada measurement and size guide: https://ca.bauer.com/pages/size-guide-shin-pads
* Published measurement: centre of kneecap to centre of outer ankle.
* That page links `size-chart-shin-pads-youth.csv`, `size-chart-shin-pads-junior.csv`, `size-chart-shin-pads-intermediate.csv`, and `size-chart-shin-pads-senior.csv` under https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0658/4749/2861/files/ (version 1749573661).
* Canadian product snapshot: https://ca.bauer.com/collections/hockey-shin-pads/products.json?limit=100

Metric chart intervals used in the module:

| Product size | Leg measurement, cm |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | 25.5 to 28 |
| 9 | 28 to 31 |
| 10 | 31 to 33.5 |
| 11 | 33.5 to 36 |
| 12 | 36 to 38.5 |
| 13 | 38.5 to 41 |
| 14 | 41 to 43.5 |
| 15 | 43.5 to 46 |
| 16 | 46 to 48.5 |
| 17 | 48.5 to 51.5 |
| 18 | 51.5 and above |

Exact interval boundaries produce both adjacent starting sizes. Product size is not a direct inch conversion of the measurement. Inputs are limited to 15 through 65 cm; measurements below the chart produce no guessed size. Tongue position does not automatically change size. Other brands require their own verified chart.

The catalog includes only non-street Bauer models, with size-specific CAD prices and stock from the snapshot. Only available variants in the matched size and within budget are shown. Tax and shipping are excluded. Refresh shin-data.js from the official source to update price and availability; the browser does not fetch a live catalog. No popularity or review ranking is claimed. Playing level changes fitting advice, not a claim that every model provides sufficient protection for a competition level.

The original generated tongue illustration is illustrative, not a scale measurement diagram. The measurement step links to Bauer's own diagram. Fit checks supplement physical store fitting.

## Photos and privacy

Optional front and side photos can use a browser camera dialog with getUserMedia, preferring the rear camera, or a separate image file picker. The camera dialog provides framing prompts, a preview, retake and use controls. Camera permission and a secure context are required; failures direct the user to permission settings or the file picker. All stream tracks are stopped after capture, closing, leaving the tool and pagehide, including streams returned after a pending permission request was cancelled. Canvas data is cleared when closing. Blob URLs display local previews. No photo is transmitted, written to localStorage, or stored on a server. URLs are revoked on clear, leaving the store-check page, switching equipment, and pagehide. The app does not control an original photo already stored in the user's device gallery.

The result depends on four explicit answers: knee cup position, coverage, pad security, and movement. Any negative answer prompts adjustment; missing or uncertain answers require further checks. Four positive answers produce a qualified self-check result.
