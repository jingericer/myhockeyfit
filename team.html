A BETTER FIT.

Join a small volunteer team to make hockey equipment choices clearer for players and families, especially those new to the game.

Started by Eric J · U12 hockey player in Kanata

Why I started this

Choosing a stick or shin guards can be confusing when you are starting hockey. I made MyHockeyFit as a free guide for our community. Now I’m looking for a few people who want to help test it, improve it and share it with other families.

Ways to help

You don’t need to be a programmer to join.

✓

Test and give feedback

Try the stick and shin guard guides. Tell us what’s confusing and check whether the advice makes sense in a real store.

⌘

Build and improve the site

Help make the mobile experience clearer, fix bugs and work on new features. Small contributions are welcome.

↗

Help hockey families find it

Share the project with interested families and coaches. Help write simple explanations for beginners.

Start small

We’ll begin with a small group and a four week trial. Volunteer when you have time. Eric will collect feedback and share what the team improves.

Everyone gets credit

With your permission, we’ll recognize your contribution to the project. For youth volunteers, a parent or guardian should be involved in joining and communications.

Interested in joining?

Tell us whether you would like to test, build or help share MyHockeyFit. No hockey experience is required for testing the beginner experience.

How would you like to help?

We’ll use the parent email only to follow up about this volunteer project. Please don’t include a child’s full name.

Thank you for joining in.

We’ll contact your parent or guardian about next steps.
