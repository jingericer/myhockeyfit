How HockeyFit makes this recommendation

HockeyFit begins with the common weight based flex guideline, then adjusts for a player's age, experience, competition level, preferred feel. Length starts in the chin to nose fitting zone while wearing skates, then considers position and puck control preference. Release feel guides kick point comparisons. Blade preference is a separate choice; no shot type determines either one.

Sources: Bauer Flex & Measurement Guide, Bauer, CCM, Warrior, TRUE, Sherwood, and the HockeyMonkey fitting guide.

This is a fitting guide, not a guarantee. Before cutting or buying, hold the exact stick in skates and confirm the lie, shaft length and ability to load it with a coach or experienced fitter.
